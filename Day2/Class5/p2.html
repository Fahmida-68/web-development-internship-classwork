Chicken Biryani

Time: 1 hour | Portion: 4 people

Ingredients

  • Rice
    • Basmati Rice - 500 grams
  • Chicken
    • Chicken - 1 kg
    • Yogurt - 1 cup
  • Spices
    • Cardamom, Cinnamon, Cloves
    • Biryani Masala - 2 tsp

Instructions

  1. Chal 30 minutes vijiye rakhun
  2. Chicken marinate korun
  3. Chicken cook korun
  4. Rice 70% cook korun
  5. Layer kore dome rakhun 25minutes
